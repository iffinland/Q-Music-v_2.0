# Q-Music — quick guide

## Choose your look

Open **Appearance** in the left menu. Classic keeps the familiar spacious layout;
Modern uses compact cards and scrolling Home rows. The choice is remembered on
this device. The light/dark control is separate. Switching does not stop music.
Modern's sidebar can collapse to icons; mobile uses the menu drawer.

## Browse and open details

Home shows recent music. Browse All and Playlists offer filters, alphabetical
sorting and numbered pages. Library and My Music use the same card style.
Open a card's details to read its full metadata; close or use Back to return to
your collection. **Published @ Q-Music** and **Published @ Ear-Bump** identify the
publishing app. Original Ear-Bump content remains read-only in Q-Music.

Tap an artist or publisher name on a card, in details or in the mini-player to
open that collection. Artist credits are names supplied with publications, not
verified artist accounts. Search more songs finds additional artist matches.

## Search and discover

Use the Home search box or **Search** in the menu. Submit your words, then choose
All, Songs, Playlists, Artists or Publishers. Song/playlist results match available
indexed details; creator tabs match names. Results say when only part of the
index has been checked. **Search more music** continues in small batches.

**Discover Mix** finds up to 20 recent/older indexed songs. Tap **Find a mix**,
preview, then **Play mix** or Play on one card. **Find another mix** keeps your
current playback until you choose to play the new selection. It samples music
known to your node; it is not an endless radio stream or a full QDN scan.

## Player controls

Use the progress slider to seek, and the speaker icon for volume/mute. Repeat
cycles Off, Repeat queue and Repeat one. Next still advances when Repeat one is
selected. Shuffle chooses from the current queue; Previous follows your actual
shuffle history. Pause, seeking and volume continue to work while browsing.
An unavailable file may need Retry or Next; the app cannot create missing data.

## Library and publishing

Save songs/playlists from details. Library belongs to your current wallet/name;
Save to QDN is an explicit backup action. Saving a favorite does not mirror audio.

Publish Audio offers separate song, album/multiple-file and playlist flows.
File/cover hints appear there, and **Help** in the menu contains format and size
information. Review before approving Hub's publishing prompts. If interrupted,
use Publish activity to check/resume the existing job rather than creating a
new publication. The 64 MiB edit-reuse threshold does not limit normal listening
or publishing; larger existing audio requires selecting the original file when
editing.

## Mirror through your own node

Open an artist/publisher collection, then **Mirror publisher** or **Mirror artist
publishers**. Artist choices include only publishers found so far; finish more
search batches first if needed. Select up to 20 publishers, acknowledge the
scope and ownership of the selected node, then **Follow on my node**. Hub asks
for permission; Q-Music reads the saved rule back. Nothing is selected by default.

Following covers **all content** from those publishers, including non-music files
and future publications. Total size is unknown. Use your own node, keep it
selected in Hub during approval, and make sure its storage policy permits followed
content (FOLLOWED, FOLLOWED_OR_VIEWED or ALL). Capacity, availability and blocked
content still matter. The node can manage downloads after closing Q-Music;
a saved follow does not certify all downloads are complete or permanently pinned.

**Manage all Q-Music follows** shows this app's saved rules. Select existing rules
and **Stop Q-Music follow** to remove them. This does not delete downloaded files
or change another app's follow lists. Other node settings may keep hosting them.
Public nodes cannot be managed. After an uncertain result, **Refresh status**
checks the saved state without resending a write. No QORT payment is involved.
